# Stirling Generator
<BlockImage id="enderio:stirling_generator" scale="4" />

Combining a <ItemLink id="enderio:void_chassis" /> with a furnace, obsidian, and some <ItemLink id="enderio:iron_gear" />s creates the **Stirling Generator**, an early-game source of energy. 

<Recipe id="stirling_generator" />

The generator burns fuel like a furnace, converting it to μI. Improved capacitors will increase the energy generation rate and decrease the fuel consumption rate.

* **Basic Capacitor:** Generates 40μI/tick at 100% efficiency.
* **Double-Layer Capacitor:** Generates 80μI/tick at 120% efficiency.
* **Octadic Capacitor:** Generates 120μI/tick at 140% efficiency.

Despite the name, the Stirling Generator does *not* create Scottish cities.
